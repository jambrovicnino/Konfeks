Our Story

Built on Family.
Driven by Trust.

For over 30 years, KONFEKS D.O.O. has been a cornerstone of reliability in logistics and retail. Founded in 1993 as a family enterprise, we have grown from a single-truck operation into a diversified business spanning road freight, cargo handling, and specialised retail.

What has never changed is our commitment to the values that built this company: the personal touch of a family business, combined with the professionalism of an industry leader.
